About Kala Vedika

A sanctuary for classical arts and cultural expression

Founded in 2025, Kala Vedika has been the premier cultural organization at KITS, dedicated to preserving and promoting the rich heritage of classical Indian arts.

We believe that art is not just performance — it is a language that transcends boundaries, connects generations, and speaks to the soul. Our organization provides a platform for students to discover their artistic talents and present them to the world.
